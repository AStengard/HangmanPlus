📖 How The Page Works

Step 1

🎯 The Main Goal

A secret word has been intercepted in orbit. Your objective is to guess the missing letters using your keyboard or the on-screen console. Solving the word pulls your spacewalking astronaut safely back toward the station airlock.

Step 2

⚡ Watch Your Tethers (Lives)

You have 6 Energy Tethers holding the astronaut to the hull. Every incorrect letter guess snaps 1 tether, causing the astronaut to drift farther away into deep space. If all 6 tethers snap, the astronaut is lost and your run ends.

Step 3

💡 Read The Clues

Look at the Tiered Signal Clues box on the left:

  • Tier 1 (Broad): Always available right from the start of each word.
  • Tier 2 (Medium): Automatically decrypts if you reach ≤3 remaining tethers.
  • Tier 3 (Critical): A final specific hint that unlocks when you are down to your last cable.
Step 4

🔋 Charge & Lifelines (Hotkeys 1, 2, 3)

Correct letter guesses fill your ⚡ Charge Meter (rare letters like Q, X, Z give bonus charge!). Spend charge on 3 emergency lifelines:

  • 🧹 Sweep (Key [1]): Removes 3 wrong letters from the keyboard console (Costs 40⚡).
  • 📡 Sonar (Key [2]): Automatically reveals 1 hidden vowel in the word (Costs 60⚡).
  • 🛡️ Shield (Key [3]): Blocks your next mistake with zero tether loss and protects your streak (Costs 80⚡).
  • 🎯 Solve Now (Spacebar): Type the whole word anytime. Solve with 4+ blanks remaining for a +1,000 PTS Early Solve Bonus!
Step 5

🚀 Endless Run & Ship Modules

In this Endless Run mode, every word you clear heals +2 Tethers. Every 3 cleared words, you draft 1 permanent Rogue-lite ship upgrade (such as free Vowel Sonar, 7 max tethers, or auto-revealed starting letters) to help you push your high score!
